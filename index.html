How a Project Ships

From survey pin to front-door key.

The same four-stage sequence runs on every Meridian project, regardless of scale.

01 / Survey

Land & Feasibility

Topographic survey, soil testing and market feasibility, typically 6–10 weeks before design begins.

02 / Design

Master Planning

Architecture, structural engineering and MEP design finalized and permitted before the first excavation.

03 / Build

Construction

Quarterly third-party inspections published to buyers throughout the build cycle.

04 / Deliver

Handover

Unit inspection walkthrough with the buyer, snag-list resolution, then keys — on the contracted date.
